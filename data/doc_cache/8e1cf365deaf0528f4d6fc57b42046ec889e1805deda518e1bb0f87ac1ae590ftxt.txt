Backend Engineer

Role: Backend Engineer
Experience level: ~4 years
Stack: Python, distributed systems, REST/gRPC APIs, Postgres, Redis, cloud infra (AWS or GCP)

Responsibilities:
- Design and build backend services handling high request volume (10k+ req/s at peak)
- Work on distributed systems problems: rate limiting, caching, consistency, concurrency
- Debug production issues independently, including performance and memory problems
- Participate in system design discussions and justify tradeoffs

Required competencies:
1. System design — can design a service under real constraints (throughput, latency, consistency), and adapt the design when constraints change.
2. Concurrency & correctness — understands race conditions, locking strategies, and Python-specific concurrency behavior (GIL, threading vs multiprocessing vs asyncio).
3. Debugging methodology — can describe a structured approach to diagnosing a production issue (e.g., a memory leak), grounded in real tools and real experience.
4. Tradeoff reasoning — can articulate why one approach was chosen over another, including the downsides of their own chosen approach.
